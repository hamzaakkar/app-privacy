## About this app

Soccer League Memory 2026 Tips is an independent Free Fire fan companion with a football memory beginner guide, quiz, practice spinner, radar simulation, bundle cost calculator and a separate football picture memory game with 20 levels. It does not connect to Free Fire, detect real players, convert skins or award currency or items in other games. It is not affiliated with Garena, football players, clubs or organizations pictured.

## Accounts and app information

The memory game does not require an account, sign-in or payment. Its game code does not request your name, contact list, photos, microphone, camera or precise GPS location. Bundled images are app assets, not images collected from your device. External websites have their own accounts, terms and privacy practices.

## On-device information

Football memory unlocked levels, best move counts and bonus stars are saved in Android app preferences. Quiz answers, calculator inputs, spinner results, radar state and current card selections are held in memory and are not uploaded by the app. Local saved records remain until cleared or removed; operating-system backups or device transfers may include preferences according to your device settings. Bundled player photos are app assets, not photos collected from your device.

## Advertising and Unity Ads

When the public GitHub adsactive switch enables advertising, Unity Ads initializes automatically. There is no in-app Accept/Reject screen. Banners may appear in memory screens; interstitials are requested after each completed memory level, every third completed quiz, and every third feature or practice action. Optional completed rewarded ads grant an in-app preview, bonus star or practice tip; skips and failures grant no bonus. Unavailable ads do not block normal use. The app requests non-personalized advertising. Unity may process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, device details and ad interactions for delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. IP addresses can indicate approximate location. See https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy for Unity and partner processing.

## Remote settings

The app requests public feature settings hosted on GitHub at startup, on resume and about every 60 seconds while open. GitHub receives technical connection information such as IP address and request details. The adsactive and cpaactive flags independently enable advertising and sponsored buttons. No scores, card selections or earned bonus stars are included in those requests. Invalid or unavailable settings disable ads and sponsored buttons until a successful refresh.
