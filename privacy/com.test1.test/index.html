About this app

test1 is an independent picture memory matching game with 20 levels and bundled card and background images. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the people, clubs or organizations pictured. It does not connect to other games or award items in other games.

Accounts and app information

The memory game does not require an account, sign-in or payment. Its game code does not request your name, contact list, photos, microphone, camera or precise GPS location. Bundled images are app assets, not images collected from your device. External websites have their own accounts, terms and privacy practices.

On-device information

Unlocked levels, best move counts and earned bonus stars are saved in Android app preferences. Card selections and round state are kept in memory during play. The game does not send your matches or scores to the developer or an app game server. Local records remain until app data is cleared or removed; operating-system backup or device transfer may include app preferences depending on device settings, and may restore them. Manage those backups through your device or backup provider. Remote feature flags are held in app memory.

Advertising and Unity Ads

When the GitHub adsactive setting enables advertising, Unity Ads initializes automatically; this version has no in-app Accept/Reject ad screen. Banners may appear on app screens and interstitials are requested after a completed level. Optional rewarded ads provide a short card preview or one bonus star per level only after completion. Unity may process IP address, device and advertising identifiers, operating-system and device details, and ad interactions for delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. IP addresses can indicate approximate location. The app requests non-personalized advertising; this does not prevent all technical processing. Unity and its partners describe their processing and choices in their policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Remote settings

The app requests public feature settings hosted on GitHub at startup, on resume and about every 60 seconds while open. GitHub receives technical connection information such as IP address and request details. The adsactive and cpaactive flags independently enable advertising and sponsored buttons. No scores, card selections or earned bonus stars are included in those requests. Invalid or unavailable settings disable ads and sponsored buttons until a successful refresh.

External and sponsored links

Get Gifts opens a sponsored external offer only when you tap it and when sponsored links are enabled. Tracking redirects and attribution parameters may be used. A destination may receive connection information, use cookies and request information, registration or payment under its own privacy policy. Review the destination and its terms before sharing anything. Offers do not guarantee prizes. Opening offers is not required to play or unlock levels. In-app trophies and bonus stars are separate from external offers. Photo-source and privacy links also open external websites only when tapped.

Third-party services and policy hosting

Unity (when advertising is configured), GitHub for remote settings and this policy page, and external destinations operate under their own policies. GitHub may process technical connection information when you visit this page. GitHub privacy policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Retention and international processing

Local game preferences remain until cleared or removed, subject to operating-system backup and restore settings. Third-party providers determine retention for data they control, including advertising, security and legal records, and may process information internationally. See their policies for retention and privacy-request procedures. The developer cannot promise deletion of information controlled by another provider.

Parents and guardians should supervise younger users, especially advertising and external destinations. Do not provide a child’s personal information through sponsored links. Contact us about child privacy concerns; applicable child privacy protections and third-party rules apply.

Your device provides advertising and privacy controls whose effects depend on the platform and provider. Closing the app stops new app activity. Non-personalized advertising still involves technical processing.

Withdrawal of consent

Where a provider relies on your consent, use that provider's available privacy controls or contact it to withdraw consent. You can contact the developer about app-related processing, use device privacy controls, avoid optional ads and external offers, or stop using the online features.
